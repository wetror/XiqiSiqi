🏢 About Us

Shenzhen XiqiSiqi Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.

🔷 Company Profile

Shenzhen XiqiSiqi Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is a leading professional wholesale supplier of mobile accessories and communication peripherals. We specialize in mobile accessories, 3C electronics, and OEM/ODM custom services.

🔷 Brand Story

With the widespread adoption of smartphones, accessories have evolved into core peripherals. XiqiSiqi was founded on an uncompromising commitment to quality and innovation to serve global retailers.

🔷 Business Philosophy

  • Quality First: Strict inspection from raw materials to shipment.
  • Win-Win: Competitive wholesale pricing and flexible terms.
  • Agile Innovation: Keeping pace with major brand releases.

🔷 History

  • 2020: Established company and initial wholesale warehouse.
  • 2022: Expanded factory partnerships and QA inspection teams.
  • 2023: Launched digital B2B online wholesale procurement platform.
  • 2025: Integrated WMS warehouse automation for fast delivery.
  • Present: Serving over 2,000+ physical stores worldwide.

🔷 Quality Assurance

All items pass strict quality control protocols:

  • Safety Standards: Certified protection circuits against overcharge and short circuits.
  • Precision Molds: 1:1 authentic phone mold design for perfect fit.
  • Rigorous Tests: Drop-tested armor cases and 9H tempered glass.
